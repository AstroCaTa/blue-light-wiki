# Blue-light filtering spectacles probably make no difference to eye strain, eye health or sleep quality

- Source: Cochrane News (University of Melbourne / Cochrane Eyes and Vision)
- URL: https://www.cochrane.org/about-us/news/blue-light-filtering-spectacles-probably-make-no-difference-eye-strain-eye-health-or-sleep
- Published: 2023-07-26

## Key Findings
- A Cochrane systematic review analyzed **17 randomised controlled trials** across six countries (including Australia, the Czech Republic, Japan, Norway, the USA, and the UK), with participant counts ranging from 5 to 156 and assessment durations from less than one day to five weeks.
- **Eye strain:** Blue-light filtering spectacle lenses probably make no difference to short-term eye strain caused by computer use compared with non-blue-light filtering lenses.
- **Visual acuity & sleep:** There is little or no effect on best-corrected visual acuity, and the effect on sleep-related measures is inconclusive and unclear.
- **Retinal health:** No conclusions could be drawn about potential long-term effects on retinal health due to short follow-up periods.

## Optical & Exposure Context
- According to first author Dr. Sumeer Singh, the amount of blue light our eyes receive from artificial sources such as computer screens is about **a thousandth (1/1000)** of what we get from natural daylight.
- Standard blue-light filtering lenses typically filter out about **10–25%** of blue light, depending on the product.
- Filtering out higher levels of blue light would require lenses with an obvious amber tint, which would substantially alter color perception.

## Clinical Practice & Side Effects
- A 2018 Australian survey of 372 optometrists found that **75%** prescribed blue-light filtering lenses despite acknowledging limitations in supporting evidence.
- Infrequent, mild, and temporary adverse effects included discomfort wearing glasses, headaches, and lower mood—similar to non-blue-light filtering spectacles.
- Senior author Prof. Laura Downie concluded that current evidence does not support prescribing blue-light filtering lenses to the general population.
